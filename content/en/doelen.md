## Project goals

We're developing and testing an AI method that does the following.

1. **Generate plain-language Dutch summaries**, tailored to healthcare psychologists, care professionals, policy officers and other non-academic professionals.
2. **Use open or publicly governed language models where possible**, such as [WiLLMa / GPT-NL](https://www.gpt-nl.nl/) via the [SURF AI Hub](https://www.surf.nl/en/themes/artificial-intelligence/projects-and-collaborations/ai-hub).
3. **Be fully transparent and reproducible**: open prompts, open code, a documented pipeline and evaluation method.
4. **Stay close to the source text**, with as few hallucinations as possible and preserved nuance.
5. **Be scalable** for libraries and content platforms.

This narrows the gap between open access and real accessibility, and strengthens the role of libraries as trusted intermediaries.
