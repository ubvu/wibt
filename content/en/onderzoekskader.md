## Research context

A few findings from the literature that underpin this project.

**Readability.** Several studies show that language models often produce more readable summaries than researchers themselves.

**Factuality and bias.** Models sometimes hallucinate or over-generalize. Caution is needed around subtle or uncertain findings.

**Human plus AI works best.** Language models produce a good first draft. Experts correct nuance and errors.

**Methodology.** Multi-agent workflows and advanced evaluation methods have a large effect on quality.

*Based on the presentation "State of the Art in LLM-Generated Lay Summaries of Scientific Articles."*
