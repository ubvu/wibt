## Motivation

Open access makes research articles freely available. That doesn't make them understandable.

Roughly 40% of open-access readers are non-academic: teachers, healthcare professionals, policymakers, citizens. They get stuck on jargon, long sentences and abstract phrasing. Disinformation doesn't have that problem. It spreads easily precisely because it's written in simple, appealing language.

Meanwhile, researchers are increasingly asked to demonstrate societal impact and make their work accessible to a broad audience. Writing a good plain-language summary takes time and a distinct skill.

Generative AI can help, but most existing tools aren't transparent, aren't always reliable, and often depend on a handful of big tech companies. We're researching an open, auditable, public alternative.

*Source: Wirsching et al. (2020), [Open for All: Exploring the reach of open access content to non-academic audiences](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4143313).*
