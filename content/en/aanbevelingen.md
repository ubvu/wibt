## Recommendations

- **Keep a human in the loop.** Language models remain error-prone. An author or subject-matter expert checks the content before a summary is published or used.
- **Label AI-generated text.** Every summary gets a clearly visible "AI-generated" label. That's also what the EU AI Act, Article 50, has required for published AI content since August 2026.
- **Count the energy cost.** The pipeline uses a fair amount of compute per summary. At scale, across many articles, that's a real concern.
- **Improve the translation step.** Some readability complaints arose during translation into Dutch. Follow-up research looks at Dutch language models such as GPT-NL/WiLLMa to avoid unnatural sentences.
- **Find a better measure for readability.** Because people disagree with each other, measuring readability more objectively is a concrete next step.
- **This is a method, not a ready-made service.** Organizations that want to adopt the approach build further on the open code and prompts themselves. There is no production-ready, plug-and-play solution.
